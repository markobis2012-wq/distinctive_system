Methodology

The Asset Corporate Sustainability Leadership Awards – Jade, Platinum, Gold and Titanium – comprise the longest running corporate sustainability benchmarking service in Asia. For over two decades, companies have used the process to evaluate and highlight their sustainability credentials.

Each award is based on the online submission covering a wide-range of metrics, including waste and water management, energy consumption and GHG emissions, supplier codes of conduct, sustainability financing and corporate governance. The submissions are reviewed, checked and analyzed in detail by the Editorial Board. Members of the Editorial Board interview contenders for the Jade award to investigate the depth of their commitment to sustainablity and the scope and effect of the policies. In addition, companies are evaluated against their peers across various sectors and markets.

Note: Companies are listed in alphabetical order within each award category.
